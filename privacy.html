Privacy Notice

How we look after the personal information you share with us.

Who we are

The Friends of Derby Arboretum (FDA) is a registered charity (no. 1176067) based at Loudon Lodge, 57 Morelston Street, Derby, DE23 8FL.

What we collect

When you use the form on our Contact page, we collect your name, email address, the subject you choose and your message. If you email us directly, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

How we use it

We use your information solely for the legitimate interests of the FDA in order to deal with your enquiry, for example to answer a question, arrange a Loudon Lodge booking or respond to a membership or volunteering request.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with any other organisation.

How the contact form works

Messages sent through our contact form are delivered to us by Formspree, a form-handling service, which forwards them to the Friends' email inbox.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries only for as long as we need them to deal with your request, and then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it at any time. To do so, please contact us.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
